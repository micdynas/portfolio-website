About Me

Hello, I'm Michael Orji

I am a Mechatronics Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology Owerri with a passion for frontend development, automation, and emerging technologies. I enjoy turning ideas into clean, user-friendly interfaces and solving real-world problems through technology. My background in Mechatronics has given me an interest in understanding how hardware and software can work together to create useful and intelligent systems. This has influenced the way I approach development: I enjoy not only building things that work, but also understanding the problem they are intended to solve.

My Journey

My interest in technology has led me to explore several areas, particularly web development and engineering. I began developing my skills in frontend development by learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and applying them through practical projects. Rather than focusing only on tutorials, I have tried to learn by building real interfaces, experimenting with different designs, debugging problems, and continuously improving my projects. Through these projects, I have developed an appreciation for responsive design, user experience, clean code, and creating interfaces that are both functional and intuitive. I also use Git and GitHub to manage my projects and document my development work.

Engineering Experience

During my SIWES internship at JRB Solar Investments Limited, I gained hands-on experience in the Solar Systems Installation and Maintenance department. I assisted with more than 10 solar power system installations and gained practical exposure to solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, electrical connections, system testing, and maintenance procedures. The experience helped me develop practical problem-solving skills and taught me the importance of accuracy, safety, teamwork, and following established procedures when working with technical systems. Working in an engineering environment also strengthened my ability to troubleshoot problems, document technical activities, and learn from more experienced professionals.

Exploring Artificial Intelligence

As I continue developing my frontend skills, I have become increasingly interested in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and automation. I am currently learning Python and building my understanding of the foundations required for working with intelligent systems. My goal is to combine my existing software development skills with my engineering background to eventually build applications that can automate tasks, analyze information, and solve practical problems. One of the projects I am currently developing is SolarSense AI, which combines my interest in renewable energy, software development, and artificial intelligence. I see AI and automation as a natural extension of my engineering background, and I am actively working toward developing the skills required to work in this field.

How I Approach Projects

I enjoy learning by building. When working on a project, I try to understand the problem first before thinking about the implementation. I break larger problems into smaller components, research unfamiliar concepts, experiment with different approaches, and troubleshoot issues until I understand what went wrong. I also believe that good development is not only about writing code. A useful application should be accessible, responsive, intuitive, and designed around the people who will actually use it.
